About

PARIS 48.8566° N · SEOUL 37.5665° N

Madiha holds an advanced LLM in International and European Law, specialising in human rights, security, and development, and is currently preparing for the French Bar Examination (CRFPA) in Paris.

Her work sits at the junction of international law, climate policy, and institutional communications — built across legal advisory work in France and a communications posting with a climate and green-growth institution in Seoul. She translates dense policy and legal material into content that institutional, diplomatic, and public audiences can actually use.

Languages: French (C2), English (C2), Spanish (B2).
